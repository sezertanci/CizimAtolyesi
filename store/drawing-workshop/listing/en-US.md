## Uygulama adı (en fazla 30 karakter)

Drawing Workshop

## Kısa açıklama (en fazla 80 karakter)

A safe, ad-free drawing and colouring app for kids

## Tam açıklama (en fazla 4000 karakter)

Drawing Workshop is a drawing and colouring app where children bring their imagination to life with colour.

Draw freely with a variety of pencils, brushes and paints.
Colour ready-made pages, create patterns with symmetry and add stickers.
Drawings are saved automatically and are always waiting in the gallery.

No ads, no accounts and no in-app purchases. No personal data is collected, drawings stay on the device and no internet connection is needed.
